PRO TIPS

master prompt engineering

get better results with these simple techniques

1

Be Specific

Instead of "a car", try "a red Ferrari 458 Italia, sunset lighting, highway background, cinematic shot"

"a dog"
"a golden retriever puppy playing in autumn leaves, golden hour lighting"
2

Add Style Keywords

Include art styles, camera terms, or moods: "photorealistic", "oil painting", "cyberpunk", "4k", "trending on ArtStation"

"fantasy castle, digital art, epic lighting, highly detailed, 8k"
3

Use Adjectives

Describe colors, textures, emotions: "majestic", "gloomy", "vibrant", "ethereal", "warm", "dark"

"a mystical forest with glowing mushrooms, ethereal atmosphere, bioluminescent plants"
4

Composition Matters

Specify angles: "close-up", "wide shot", "aerial view", "portrait", "from behind"

"mountain landscape, wide angle, dramatic clouds, sunrise, national geographic style"
5

Negative Prompts

Tell AI what to avoid: "no people", "without text", "no watermark", "not blurry"

"beautiful beach, no people, sunset, clean composition"
6

Combine Concepts

Mix unexpected elements for unique results: "steampunk", "cyberpunk", "fantasy", "sci-fi"

"cyberpunk samurai in neon city, rain, reflective armor, blade runner aesthetic"
